Capabilities

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Covo is engineered to handle the small stuff so you can focus on the big stuff.

🎙️

Voice Command Control

Talk to Covo naturally. No rigid commands or apps required — just say what you need and Covo figures out the rest. Powered by an on-device language model for instant, private responses.

📦

Carry & Deliver

Covo can carry up to 12 lbs and navigate every room in your home. Send it to grab your charger, bring snacks to the couch, or deliver medications to an elderly family member upstairs.

🧠

Routine Learning

Covo watches how you live and adapts. After a week, it starts anticipating — bringing your morning coffee, dimming lights at bedtime, or reminding you of your evening meds.

🗺️

Smart Navigation

3D LiDAR + depth cameras build a real-time map of your home. Covo avoids furniture, pets, and kids — even as your layout changes.

🔒

Private by Design

All AI processing happens on-device. Your routines, voice data, and home layout never leave Covo. No cloud required, no subscription surveillance.

🔋

All-Day Battery

14-hour runtime on a single charge. Covo docks itself automatically when low and is back at your side before you need it — no babysitting required.
